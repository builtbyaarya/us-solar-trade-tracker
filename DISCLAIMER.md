# Not legal or trade advice

This repository is a student-maintained reference compiled from public documents. It is not legal,
trade, customs, tax or investment advice, and it is not an official source.

- Antidumping and countervailing duty rates change through preliminary and final determinations,
  orders, administrative reviews, scope rulings and court remands. A rate in this dataset may have
  been superseded by the time you read it.
- Cash deposit rates, assessment rates and the rate that applies to a specific entry can differ.
- Factory capacity figures are what companies and public bodies announced; announced capacity is
  often not built, is built later than announced, or is built smaller.

Verify every number against the linked source before you rely on it. If you find an error, open an
issue with the source document and we will fix it.

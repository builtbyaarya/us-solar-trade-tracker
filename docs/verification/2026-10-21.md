## Part C. 14-21 Oct 2026 developments on the India / Indonesia / Laos case

What happened:

1. USITC final affirmative injury vote, 14 Oct 2026 (News Release 26-147, Inv. 701-TA-772-774 and 731-TA-1756-1758). Vote 5-0 affirmative (Doyle, Kearns, Pappas, Thanhauser, Foley); Commissioner Negatu not participating. Finding: material injury for all three countries (no threat finding). Critical circumstances (release updated 15 Oct): India negative, Laos negative, Indonesia affirmative, each "for certain imports" (the imports Commerce found critical circumstances on). USITC Publication 5804 due by 23 Nov 2026.
2. Commerce orders not yet published. Petitioners' counsel (Brightbill) expects the orders on 2 Nov 2026; pv magazine says "early November". A Federal Register API search for "photovoltaic" with publication dates 1-21 Oct 2026 returns only two unrelated documents, so no order, amended final determination or ITC FR notice had been published by 21 Oct.
3. No amended final determinations or ministerial-error notices for India, Indonesia or Laos were found in the FR or the trade press.
4. Related items in the same news cycle, outside the case itself: petitioners say Ethiopia is now the third-largest import source (imports up >3,000% in 12 months) and a Commerce inquiry is under way; the Section 232 polysilicon tariff takes effect 4 Dec 2026 (Utility Dive, 15 Oct 2026).
